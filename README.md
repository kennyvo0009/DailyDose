# DailyDose

Paste your notes and get a cram summary, the key idea, or a quick quiz.

## How to run it

https://kennyvo0009.github.io/DailyDose/

## How it works

The words your notes repeat the most are probably what the test is about.

- **Cram Summary**: the top half of your lines, most important first.
- **Key Idea**: the most repeated words and the most important line.
- **Quick Quiz**: multiple choice questions from lines like "Term: meaning".
